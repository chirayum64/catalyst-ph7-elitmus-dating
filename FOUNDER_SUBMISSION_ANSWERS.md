## 5. Form Question: "What legal, privacy, and safety implications did you consider and implement?"

**Answer:**
1. **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 — Purpose Limitation & Explicit Consent Firewall:**
   Under India's DPDP Act 2023, personal data collected for *employment evaluation* (the pH Test) cannot be legally repurposed for a *dating service* without explicit, granular, informed opt-in consent.
   - **Our Solution:** `pH7` never auto-publishes any eLitmus candidate profile. Users must explicitly opt in via the **Cryptographic Consent Ledger** during `Import eLitmus pH ID`. Furthermore, **raw pH test scores and hiring evaluations are strictly firewalled** and never exposed on `pH7`; only a one-way, non-sensitive *Cognitive Archetype* is transferred.
2. **POSH Act (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) & Corporate Client Insulation:**
   eLitmus's primary asset is trust with corporate HR teams. If employees harassed coworkers from the same company on an eLitmus dating app, it would create POSH liability and B2B churn.
   - **Our Solution:** **Mandatory-Default Coworker Domain Firewall** (`blockCurrentCompany: true`) mutually cloaks users sharing the same corporate email domain (e.g., `@razorpay.com`) and blocks corporate recruiter accounts from accessing `pH7`.
3. **IT Rules 2021 Intermediary Compliance & Offline Physical Safety:**
   - 24-hour Grievance Officer reporting pipeline & anti-harassment auto-shield.
