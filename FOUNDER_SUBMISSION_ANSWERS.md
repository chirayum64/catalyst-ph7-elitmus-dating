## 3. Product Concept & Differentiation: `Catalyst (pH7)`
- **Core Pitch:** *Dating apps profit from keeping you swiping. Catalyst earns only when verified people actually meet, in a small group, in public, within a week. Chat opens only after you've met.*
- **Why It Mirrors eLitmus's DNA:** eLitmus's hiring model is **Verify → Shortlist → Interview → Offer**. Catalyst turns that into **Verify (4-Rung Ladder) → Curated Sunday Table (4–6 people, ₹0–₹300 cap) → 2-Player Duet Puzzle & IRL Meet → Morning-After Mutual Spark**.
- **Signature Differentiators:**
  1. **Asymmetric 2-Player "Duet Puzzle" (The Wow Feature):** Before the Sunday table, two participants play a 3-minute co-op logic puzzle where each sees half the clues. It reveals how they communicate and take turns—impossible to fake with bots or AI text—and outputs a **"Duet Collaboration Note"**.
  2. **Shared-Memory pH Icebreakers (Zero Score Ranking):** With explicit consent, shows *"You both wrote the eLitmus pH test in Aug 2022 and relocated to Bengaluru"*, without ever showing or ranking test scores.
  3. **Explainable 5-Axis pH Equilibrium Radar & Life-Architecture Dilemmas:** Transparent polygon overlap across Intellectual Curiosity, Budget/Financial Thermostat, Career Ambition, Conflict Directness, and Social Battery.
  4. **Automatic Coworker & Employer Firewall:** Mutually cloaks profiles from the same corporate domain (`@razorpay.com`, `@swiggy.in`) so bosses/HR never see dating activity.
  5. **Safe-Date Co-Pilot & Live McAfee 2026 Scam Shield:** Shares a live plan link and 45m check-in timer with a trusted contact + discreet **"I Need Out" SOS button**, while the chat Scam Shield auto-blocks UPI IDs, QR requests, phone numbers, and external links (*McAfee 2026 India Research*).
